How to play & “Sell or Increase”

The basics

Four players, one 52-card deck, 13 cards each. Spades are permanent trumps. The game runs 5 rounds and the highest total score wins.

1 · Bidding

Each player secretly calls how many tricks they’ll win, from 2 to 12. All bids are revealed at once.

2 · Sell or Increase

After bids are shown, a lower bidder may offer to swap hands with a higher bidder, committing to a bigger bid: “Swap with me — I’ll bid X.” The higher bidder chooses:

  • Sell — accept the swap. The proposer takes your hand and the new bid X; you take the proposer’s hand and their original bid.
  • Increase — refuse. Your bid rises by 1 and no swap happens.

This continues around the table until the total of all bids reaches 20, or everyone passes one full round.

3 · Playing

Follow the led suit if you can. If you’re void in it, you must trump with a spade. The highest spade wins the trick; with no spades, the highest card of the led suit wins. Thirteen tricks per round.

4 · Scoring

Win exactly your call, or your call plus one, and you score your call. Miss that window and you lose your call. A big call of 8 or more that succeeds is worth a flat 13.
